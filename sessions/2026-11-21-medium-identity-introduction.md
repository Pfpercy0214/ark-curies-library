## Mechanistic revisions

1. **Identity** describes a persistent organization of a proposed medium, not necessarily an isolated, impermeable object or fixed inventory.
2. **Boundary** is an operational partition of a coupled field system. Identity and surroundings co-evolve; the same interaction is external from A's viewpoint and internal to the jointly resolved A+B system.
3. **Memory/remanence** describes retained configuration; explaining the persistence requires constitutive dynamics, energy barriers and/or slow variables. Hysteresis may still demand explicit retained state.
4. Historical **"entropy"** often meant broad unresolved environmental influence, including neighboring identities. This is not identical to thermodynamic entropy; reversible exchanges and positive entropy production require different accounts.
5. Chemical transformations and thermal contact can involve reciprocal change and concurrent opposing microscopic exchanges. Their net response must still satisfy energy and thermodynamic entropy balances. Heat transfer does not necessarily involve net material transfer of an underlying medium.
6. Magnetic flux, iron pathways, domain interactions, compensated order, and possible substrate circulation are distinguished. Conventional magnetic theory is the comparison baseline.
7. The requested universal PDE remains unspecified. A generic conservation law with moving boundary illustrates throughflow without losing organization but does not identify ARK scalars.
8. Gravity and wormhole fluid analogies remain speculative. Historical Paper VI proposed an Einstein-equation correspondence but did not prove full dynamical recovery.
