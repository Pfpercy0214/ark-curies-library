## Nonretroactivity / rights provenance

The original Aetherwave repository displays a CC BY-NC 4.0 license in `LICENSE.txt`; the original papers retain their applicable release licenses. The Library previously displayed CC0 1.0 at the root. Rights validly granted or dedicated under those terms cannot simply be revoked by later changing a root notice. Their scope and applicability may vary by file, rightsholder and publication date. The new rights-reserved provision is framed prospectively for protectable original content first published after adoption.

This is an interpretation of repository stewardship and owner intent, not a determination of the legal ownership of every file. Copyrightability and rights in AI-generated contributions are not presumed. Consultation with counsel is recommended if legal enforcement or relicensing old material is desired.
