# Investigation queue

This is a **question ledger**, not a finding ledger. Research must compare against external physics and measurements and preserve null or contrary outcomes.

1. **Neutron decay:** map historically asserted equations and assumptions to measurable weak-decay observables; identify what is independently predicted and what is supplied as input. Start only after the relevant paper is actually imported.
2. **Quantum model:** identify the versioned proposed closure and compare with the relevant QFT / Standard Model limits without treating mathematical analogy as explanatory proof.
3. **Galaxy modeling:** reconstruct versions, sources, SPARC inputs, negative results and analysis artifacts before making any new cosmic-scale claims.
4. **Environmental pressure:** distinguish positive fluid pressure, cosmological negative pressure, external confinement, and gravitational binding. Find experiments and equations able to discriminate them.
5. **Magnetic order and zero net moment:** compare ordinary antiferromagnets, altermagnets and disordered systems; distinguish equilibrium spin structure from actual transport currents.

Each investigation requires: source paths, assumptions, test specification, external measurements, outcomes, alternatives, limitations, next falsifier, and an independent review note.
