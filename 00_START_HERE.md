# Entry protocol — Ark: Curie's Library

Every AI session must start here, regardless of model or prior conversational memory.

1. Read `AGENTS.md` and `POLICIES.md` in full.
2. Read `raw/epistemics/ARK_Epistemic_Charter_v1.1.md`. For extensive work also consult `raw/epistemics/ARK_Epistemic_Charter_v1.0.md` to understand methodological changes.
3. Read `history/chronology.md`, `history/decisions.md`, `wiki/index.md`, and the most recent `sessions/` handoff, if any.
4. Check `raw/MANIFEST.json` and run the archive verifier. Do not trust a converted source until its integrity and conversion notes have been checked.
5. Declare the current task, its scientific domain, research boundary, authorized files, evidence needed, and prior model assumptions.
6. Separate established physics, ARK claims, AI deductions, computations, and open speculation. A compelling narrative is not a measurement.
7. Consult **real-world empirical and scientific evidence** before promoting explanatory claims. Source notes must preserve URLs/DOIs, accessed dates, exact supported facts, and experimental limitations.
8. Produce or update a concise session handoff. Identify what is settled, disputed, blocked, and what will falsify the next claim.

Never impersonate prior agents, pretend to have read missing documents, invent sources, or silently infer experimental validation. State limitations explicitly.

## Method authority

Canonical methodology: `raw/epistemics/ARK_Epistemic_Charter_v1.1.md` (historical copy). The current working agent protocol is `AGENTS.md` and `POLICIES.md`. If they disagree, flag the conflict for human adjudication; do not quietly overwrite the historical document.
