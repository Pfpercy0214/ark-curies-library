## Citation and academic use

Please cite the specific paper or document title, named authors, version, publication date when available, and stable URL or DOI. When drawing on the archived manuscripts, distinguish the original historical statement from current interpretations or critical reviews. Linking is preferred to copying an entire file when redistribution rights are uncertain.

Example citation format:

> Percy, P. F., Jr. (year). *Document title*, version. Aetherwave / ARK. DOI or repository URL.

Use the author list and year actually printed on the cited document, not the example's placeholder.
