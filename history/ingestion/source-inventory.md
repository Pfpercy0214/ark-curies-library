## Scope and method

- All fourteen expected standalone sources XII–XXV exist at the recorded commit. Both XXVI-labeled files also exist and are recorded below as out of scope without renumbering.
- Each cached PDF was matched to the current tree by independently recomputing its Git blob SHA-1 from `blob <byte-count>\0<bytes>`; all sixteen matches passed. SHA-256 values below were independently computed from the PDF bytes. Neither hash is a publication date or a scientific-validation claim.
- Used PyMuPDF 1.26.6 native text extraction, PDF metadata and page-count inspection, title-page rendering at 1.3 scale, visual title-sheet inspection, and additional full-size XV/XIX title checks. Extracted text was scanned across all pages for edition statements and printed-page candidates. Full substantive text, equations, tables and figures were **not** verified in this inventory.
- Exact title words and stated date/authorship are drawn from title pages, with layout line breaks collapsed. Number prefixes are listed separately. Typographic title separators are retained where explicit; a colon between a title and subtitle on separate lines is a catalog separator, not claimed source punctuation. No spelling repairs are made to author names or collaborator labels.
- `not stated` means no explicit document edition found in the inspected title/front matter or edition-keyword scan. Filename suffixes remain separate evidence; no undated paper is assigned v1. Metadata creation/modification timestamps are available in the working evidence but are **not** publication dates.
- PDF pages are 1-based file positions. All selected files have no PDF `/PageLabels` mapping. XV has printed Arabic folios 1–14, and the baryonic XXVI filename has printed “Page 1”–“Page 13”; other selected files show no systematic printed page-label sequence in this inspection. Those files are cited by PDF position, not invented printed numbers.
- Every in-scope standalone PDF has extracted substantive text on every page; its complete file extent, including references, appendices and embedded historical dialogue where present, is retained for future conversion. This does not authorize removing source dialogue or treating it as a fresh instruction.
