## Numbering and source ambiguities requiring review

1. **VI:** V2 p.1 calls its text the seventh formal entry but does not put a Roman-numeral number in the title. V2 pp.162, 192, 196 and 203 cite the same particle-identity title as Paper VI. This supports a candidate VI mapping while preserving the conflicting statement. Do not silently correct “seventh” or insert a paper number into the transcription.
2. **XI:** The last V2 paper is physically identifiable at pp.197–203, but it does not explicitly state XI. Its references run through Paper X. Placement and the expected series count alone are not definitive numbering evidence. Record as unnumbered/candidate XI pending owner confirmation or another explicit source.
3. **IX:** Its p.192 references label Biology as Paper VIII, conflicting with its IX heading. Preserve this apparent original error outside any modernized numbering scheme.
4. **II:** Preserve the two title/authorship formulations on pp.53–54. They are within one paper, not two editions to splice.
5. **VIII:** pp.158, 160 and 162 refer to “Papers VIII and VIII Pt 2”; there is no new formal paper title at those locations. The boundary inventory retains continuous pp.44–162 rather than inventing a numbered paper split.
