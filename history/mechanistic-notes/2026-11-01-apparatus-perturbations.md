# Mechanistic clarification — apparatus-induced perturbations

Recorded 2026-11-01 from the owner's current conversation. This is a paraphrase of intent, not a new empirical finding or change to the archived theory.

The owner emphasizes that active measurement and containment can physically perturb the identity and its surroundings. Especially at small scales, comparing an idealized calculation with an apparatus-conditioned result requires separately characterizing the intervention. Without that characterization, close but nonidentical results need not settle the mechanism.

Interpretive boundary: this does not authorize assigning every residual to epsilon. A change in intrinsic response, departure from a measured population, alteration of an already-produced decay product, and a detector response are distinct physical pathways. An unquantified contribution is not zero but is not an explanation either. Standard experiments already investigate many such effects.

The working requirement is to infer apparatus perturbations from independently measured conditions or calibrate a declared coupling on reference data, freeze it, and predict other observations. If a response is inverted to estimate a missing term, label it inverse inference and seek an independent comparison. Do not silently identify the total neutron disappearance rate with beta decay, or map a pressure-gauge reading directly into causal entropy.
