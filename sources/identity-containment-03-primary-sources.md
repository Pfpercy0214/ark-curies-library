## E3 — Material memory comparator

David Roylance, MIT, *Engineering Viscoelasticity*, October 24, 2001, section 3 and section 4.2; equation 29.

https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-91-mechanical-behavior-of-plastics-spring-2007/3828997b92ebb6cb70e640826be1c5f2_19_visco.pdf

Read relevant text and screenshots of PDF/printed pages 5, 12 and 13. These distinguish creep from stress relaxation and give the standard-linear-solid step response. IC-03 rewrites a single-mode conventional comparator using an internal variable and verifies its work/dissipation balance. The generalized coefficients use energy units because the chosen mode coordinate is dimensionless; they are not the source's unconverted stress moduli. This does not establish a universal material model or an ARK tau mapping.
