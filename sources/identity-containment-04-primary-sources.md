## E1 — A physical electromechanical framework

Choon Chiang Foo, Shengqiang Cai, Soo Jin Adrian Koh, Siegfried Bauer and Zhigang Suo (2012), *Model of dissipative dielectric elastomers*, Journal of Applied Physics 111, 034102. DOI 10.1063/1.3680878.

Author PDF: https://suo.seas.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum4271/files/suo/files/265.pdf

Institutional record: https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/1d1fa438-969b-4b97-9124-f43646e31fc5

Read the abstract and relevant Sections II-IV; screenshots inspected for PDF pages 3-5 (printed 034102-2 through -4). Equations 1-12 distinguish stored and leakage charge, mechanical/electrical work, and internal variables. Equations 13-18 supply an ideal-dielectric example with separate viscoelastic state. This supports using a thermodynamically constrained comparator, not identifying its variables as ARK scalars. IC-04's quadratic log-strain potential is newly specified for illustration, not that paper's Gent model or an experimental fit. Their ideal permittivity assumption illustrates why voltage at held geometry need not measure mechanical history directly.
