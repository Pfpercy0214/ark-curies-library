## M1 — Structural identifiability

Villaverde, A. F., Barreiro, A., and Papachristodoulou, A. (2016). *Structural Identifiability of Dynamic Systems Biology Models*. PLOS Computational Biology 12(10), e1005153. [DOI and original article](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005153).

**Access:** original article HTML; abstract, introduction and relevant Methods text on local observability, augmented identifiability and identifiable parameter combinations. No claim to reproduce its biological case studies or software.

**Use:** supports checking whether output information distinguishes internal parameters, and keeping local identifiability separate from global uniqueness and noise-limited precision. The present IC-01 finite-dimensional bridge calculations are our own elementary algebra, not an application of the article's full dynamic algorithm or an identifiability proof for the complete ARK PDE.

## E1 — An actual clock-ratio measurement route

Chou, C. W., Hume, D. B., Rosenband, T., and Wineland, D. J. (2010). *Optical Clocks and Relativity*. Science 329(5999), 1630-1633. DOI `10.1126/science.1192720`. [Authors' abstract and bibliographic record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20929843/).

**Access:** original research abstract via PubMed. Direct publisher full-text request returned HTTP 403; full methods, figures and raw data were not reviewed.

**Observed result reported by authors:** two optical clocks resolved motion-related time dilation below 10 m/s and a height-related effect for a separation below one metre.

**Use and limit:** establishes a real clock-comparison observable relevant to P25's proposed theta assignment. It does not measure causal stress or kappa, establish a common scalar mechanism, or supply an apparatus-specific epsilon. Before numerical use, obtain the full measurement model, corrections, reference conventions, uncertainty/covariance and data.
